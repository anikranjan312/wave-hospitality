WHO WE ARE

Wave Hospitality Group is a family-owned and operated company with over a decade of experience in managing hotels and restaurants. Our diverse portfolio spans top hotel brands and a growing number of successful food & beverage ventures. We combine operational expertise with a personal touch, ensuring every property—hospitality or restaurant—delivers quality, comfort, and consistency.

Our Story

Founded as a family business, Wave Hospitality Group has grown into a trusted name in the hospitality industry across Canada. Our journey began with a vision to create exceptional guest experiences through personalized service and operational excellence. Today, we manage a diverse portfolio of hotels and restaurants, partnering with top brands while maintaining our core values of family, integrity, and exceptional service.
