WHO WE ARE

Wave Hospitality Group is a family-owned and operated company with over a decade of experience in managing hotels and restaurants. Our diverse portfolio spans top hotel brands and a growing number of successful food & beverage ventures. We combine operational expertise with a personal touch, ensuring every property—hospitality or restaurant—delivers quality, comfort, and consistency.

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Canadian Maple Lodge has become synonymous with luxury hospitality in Canada. Our journey began with a vision to create an extraordinary retreat that seamlessly blends Canadian natural beauty with world-class luxury. Today, we stand as a testament to excellence, having hosted distinguished guests from around the globe and earning numerous accolades for our exceptional service and commitment to sustainability.
