Hotel Services

What We Offer at Wave Hospitality

We provide end-to-end operational management for hotels, overseeing daily operations, staffing, accounting, sales, and guest satisfaction. This includes everything from housekeeping to food and beverage, maintenance, and administrative support—ensuring seamless, profitable performance.

We ensure your property adheres to all franchise standards and brand guidelines, including SOPs, quality audits, guest service metrics, and visual identity requirements—avoiding penalties and maintaining brand integrity.

Our expert team uses dynamic pricing, demand forecasting, channel management, and advanced revenue management systems to maximize your hotel's profitability across all distribution platforms, including OTAs and direct bookings.

We train hotel teams on service excellence, operational efficiency, brand expectations, and leadership development. Our custom programs ensure staff are skilled, motivated, and aligned with hospitality best practices.

Restaurant Services

What We Offer at Wave Hospitality

We oversee all aspects of daily restaurant operations—staffing, kitchen workflows, inventory, guest service, and cost control. Our management ensures consistent food quality, hygiene standards, and customer satisfaction.

As experienced franchise partners, we run restaurants following brand protocols while meeting local market needs. We manage franchise relations, ensure compliance, and deliver performance aligned with brand expectations.

We manage F&B spaces within hotels or malls, handling tenant sourcing, lease agreements, and performance oversight. Our team ensures that restaurant tenants align with property goals and uphold quality standards.

Through staff training, ambiance optimization, menu curation, and service design, we create memorable dining experiences that foster repeat visits, higher reviews, and stronger brand loyalty.
